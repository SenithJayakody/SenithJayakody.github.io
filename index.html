Senith
Jayakody

Electrical and Electronic Engineering Graduate · University of Peradeniya

I currently work as an AI Research and Development Engineer at FARBE Technologies and continue my research involvement as a Volunteer Researcher at the Multidisciplinary AI Research Centre (MARC), University of Peradeniya. Previously, I served as a Research Assistant at MARC, collaborating with Prof. Roshan Godaliyadda, Prof. Parakrama Ekanayake, and Prof. Vijitha Herath.

My research interests include signal processing, statistical learning, machine learning, computational imaging, and generative modeling. My research experience spans electrohysterography signal analysis, multispectral imaging, diffusion models, and implicit neural representations, with an emphasis on rigorous evaluation and methods grounded in real-world data.
