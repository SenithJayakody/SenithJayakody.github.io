Theoretical Analysis of Diffusion Models

Investigating the statistical properties of denoising diffusion models, particularly the relationship between predicted-noise characteristics and generative fidelity. The research examines covariance structure, trace-energy behavior, and regularization strategies for improving the statistical properties of model predictions.

Diffusion ModelsGenerative AIStatistical LearningCovariance Analysis
Research details
  • Statistical analysis of predicted noise in diffusion models.
  • Investigation of covariance structure and trace-energy behavior.
  • Exploration of regularization strategies and their relationship to generative fidelity.
  • Evaluation through theoretical analysis and numerical experiments.

Computational Imaging / Generative AI
2026–Present

Image Colorization Using Implicit Neural Representations

Investigating image colorization using implicit neural representations, where continuous coordinate-based neural models reconstruct image color information under sparse color guidance. The work studies the role of image structure, color constraints, and guided optimization in recovering plausible color representations.

Implicit Neural RepresentationsImage ColorizationInverse ProblemsComputational Imaging
Research details
  • Coordinate-based implicit neural representations.
  • Image colorization formulated as an inverse problem.
  • Sparse color-hint guidance and reconstruction constraints.
  • Investigation of spatial structure and color propagation.
Manuscript under review at ICLR 2027

2025–Present

Preterm Birth Prediction From EHG and Tocographic Signals

Developing signal-processing and machine-learning methods for preterm birth classification using electrohysterography and tocographic recordings. The research investigates time-frequency representations, empirical mode decomposition, interpretable feature extraction, and robust model evaluation.

EHGSignal ProcessingEmpirical Mode DecompositionInterpretable ML
Research details
  • Spectral, statistical, entropy-based, and time-frequency features.
  • Empirical mode decomposition and intrinsic mode functions.
  • Machine-learning classification with recording-level evaluation.
  • Investigation of signal segmentation and preprocessing strategies.
Manuscript under review at PLOS ONE
